Fixing buzz and tired hands

  • Put the barre just behind the fret wire. Halfway between frets needs twice the pressure.
  • Roll the index finger slightly towards the headstock so the bony edge presses, not the soft pad.
  • Pull back with your arm weight instead of squeezing with the thumb. Thumb sits behind the neck, roughly behind your middle finger.
  • Only the strings the other fingers don’t cover need the barre. In the E-shape that is the low E, B and high e.
  • Practise on the electric first. Lighter strings and lower action make barres easier than on the J-45.
  • When the hand aches, stop and shake out. Forcing through fatigue trains tension.
